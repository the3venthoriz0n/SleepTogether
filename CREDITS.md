## Required at runtime (not included in the download)

- **Address Library for SKSE Plugins** by **meh321**, https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444. CommonLibSSE-NG resolves game addresses through it.
- **SkyUI** by the SkyUI Team, https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/12604 (settings menu).
- **MCM Helper** by **Parapets**, https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/53000. The mod's menu is defined by its `config.json`; the plugin refers to MCM Helper's `MCM_ConfigBase` script by name and ships none of its files.

## The game

- **Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition** by **Bethesda Game Studios** (a ZeniMax Media company). This mod needs the base game, and Skyrim.esm is a master of its plugin.
- The small MCM plugin `SleepTogether.esp` is generated by `tools/make_mcm_plugin.py` (or the repository's equivalent). It has `Skyrim.esm` as master and contains no gameplay records; its quest points at the MCM scripts of the mods above by name.
